Department of Computer Science & Engineering

About Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at Symbiosis Institute of Technology (SIT), Nagpur is committed to delivering high-quality education, integrating theoretical knowledge with practical applications and global exposure. Recognized among the best B.Tech. (Computer Science and Engineering) colleges in India, our goal is to equip students with cutting-edge skills and research-oriented learning, preparing them for diverse career opportunities in the tech industry, academia, and entrepreneurship.

Facilities

  • Bridging the Gap Between Academics and Industry
  • Specialization programs in emerging technologies
  • Flexi Credit courses suggested by industries
  • Industry internships and live projects
  • Value-added courses for skill enhancement
  • Global Immersion Program (GIP) for international learning experiences
  • Pathway Program to facilitate higher education opportunities abroad

Specializations & Honours Programs

  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning
  • Data Science and Analytics
  • Cloud Computing
  • Cybersecurity
  • Artificial Intelligence of Things (AIoT)
