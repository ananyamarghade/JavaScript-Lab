FESTIVAL FAQS

Essential information before stepping through the gates on November 14 in Mumbai.

AFTERHOURS 2026 is strictly an 18+ event. Valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Driver’s License, or Voter ID) will be verified at Box Office Gate 1. Digital photo copies or expired IDs cannot be accepted.

Upon completing registration, your official digital E-Ticket with a personalized QR code is generated on screen and sent to your email. Present this QR code along with your Govt ID at Box Office Turnstiles 1-4 starting at 4:30 PM IST on event day to collect your woven RFID wristband.

VIP passholders enjoy expedited express entrance lanes with zero wait time, access to the elevated panoramic VIP Mezzanine overlooking the Main Stage, air-conditioned private restrooms, a dedicated craft cocktail bar, and an official AFTERHOURS 2026 Tour Heavyweight T-Shirt & Lanyard bundle.

Small clear bags up to 12" x 6" x 12" or small waist packs are permitted. Outside food, beverages, glass bottles, professional DSLR cameras with detachable lenses, and illegal substances are strictly prohibited. Free chilled water refill stations are available across all grounds.
