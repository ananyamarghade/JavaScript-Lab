Overview

About the Department

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering at SIT Nagpur focuses on building strong programming fundamentals alongside exposure to emerging technologies. Students get hands-on experience through labs, mini projects, and industry-aligned coursework across their four years.

The department encourages a balance of academic rigour and practical exposure, preparing students for both industry roles and further studies, in line with the institute's focus on holistic, values-based engineering education.
